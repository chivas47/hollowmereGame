# Hollowmere Farm: game design

## Pitch

You take over the overgrown Hollowmere plot. Farm through the seasons, fish and forage in the Ashgrove Wilds, mine the Hollow for ore, and earn the trust of four neighbours. Original setting, characters and art; inspired by the genre, copying nothing.

## Core loop

1. Morning: wake at 6:00 with full energy and health. Sprinklers water their tiles.
2. Day: till, plant, water; forage, fish or mine; tend animals; fill machines; talk to and gift neighbours; take requests from the village quest board.
3. Ship crops, forage, fish, animal products and artisan goods in the bin (ore and stone are kept).
4. Night: sleep in the farmhouse. Watered crops grow, the bin pays out, petted animals produce, machines work.
5. Spend gold and materials at the town's shops (v46: the general store, the smithy and Bram's carpentry), the crafting menu, the barn and festival stalls.

## Time

- A day runs 6:00 to 2:00. Each 10 game minutes takes 5 real seconds (about 10 real minutes per day).
- 28-day seasons: Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter. Crops not in the new season wither.
- Passing out at 2:00 (3:00 with street lamps): wake with 60% energy.

## New game (v21)

A new farm starts on a screen that asks for the farmer's name, the farm's name (shown as "<name> Farm"), a favourite thing, colours for skin, hair, shirt and trousers, short or long hair, and a starting pet: cat, dog or turtle. The pet is saved with the profile and moves in when pets arrive (roadmap item 8). Neighbours at 2 hearts or more have a line that uses the name and the favourite thing. Old saves get the default profile: Farmer of Hollowmere Farm, the original look.

## The farmhouse (v24)

The farm door leads inside; the bed ends the day and you wake beside it. Rooms come with the house upgrades: the extended farmhouse adds a kitchen, the hillside manor a nursery, the manor with cellar (100,000g) a cellar of casks. Kitchen, nursery and cellar are furnished but do nothing yet (cooking, children and ageing come with roadmap items 16, 24 and 12).

The catalogue lectern sells 30 pieces of furniture, 12 wallpapers and 12 floorings (Stardew's furniture shop prices: chairs 300 to 750, tables 750 to 1,500, rugs 1,000 to 1,500, a tall clock 2,000; patterns 100g each). Furniture goes to the kit (slot 7) and is placed on the floor; face it to pick it up. R (or Turn in its dialog) turns a piece you placed (v99): chairs, armchairs and sofas four ways, the long rug a quarter turn, lopsided pieces mirrored; symmetrical pieces do not change. U or Ctrl+Z undoes the day's last place, pick-up or turn (20 steps). Rugs can be walked over. Doorways stay clear. The full catalogue (30,000g, as in Stardew) makes everything in it free.

Renovations at Bram's Carpentry: open-plan living room (10,000g, removes the bed corner's wall) and a reading nook (5,000g, a bay in the north wall).

## Clothing (v25)

Tailoring as in Stardew: at the sewing machine one cloth plus one item makes the piece that item belongs to. Cloth is woven there from 5 fiber (Stardew weaves cloth from wool; sheep come with roadmap item 13). Cloth is a material: kept, never shipped.

## Farm buildings: placing (v30)

Bram's Carpentry moves any building for free: back on the farm, choose the spot by facing where its top-left corner goes and pressing Use (Escape, or facing the old workbench, stops). The coop and barn take their yards and animals along; the stable takes the horse's spot. Buildings need open grass below the path (empty tilled soil is fine since v34; it is cleared), clear of crops, placed objects, debris and other buildings' yards. Grass tufts under a new building go.

### Fish pond and slime pen (v31)

## Materials from Bram (v29)

Wood 10g and stone 20g each at Bram's Carpentry, in tens and fifties (Stardew's carpenter prices). Coal stays 150g. Materials are never shipped, so buying them is a one-way cost.

## Smelting (v26)

Upgrades, buildings and recipes that took ore now take bars, at Stardew's 5 ore to a bar. The furnace (20 copper ore, 25 stone, Stardew's recipe) turns 5 copper or iron ore plus 1 coal into a copper bar (60g) or iron bar (120g). Coal (15g) comes from the charcoal kiln (10 wood each), from stone rocks in the Hollow (6% each) or from Bram at 150g (Stardew's blacksmith price). Bars and coal are materials: kept, never shipped.

## Artisan machines, part 2 (v27)

Stardew counterparts by role: keg (wine 3 x fruit, juice 2.25 x vegetable), mayonnaise machine, loom, oil maker, bee house, tapper, crystalarium, dehydrator, mushroom log and fish smoker. Ratios and night counts follow them; recipes swap hardwood and refined materials for wood, stone and bars at a similar effort. Gems: Hollow quartz 25g (2% of rocks), violet stone 100g (0.7%), Stardew's quartz and amethyst scale.

## Controls

- v129: with a second controller, press A on it to bring in a second farmer (their own look) on the same screen. They walk with the stick, go where you go when you change map, and leave with B or when their controller disconnects. Dialogs and the menu stay the first player's.
- v130: once in, the second farmer's A uses what they hold, through the same rules as yours (tools, talking, gifts, doors, map exits, which take you both), and LB and RB step through their hotbar. They have their own energy (270, refilled each morning, saved); the bag and gold are shared.
- v144: both farmers can have a dialog open at once (a shop each, the bag and a chest): each in its own half, each worked by its own player.
- v143: farmer two has their own health (the same top as yours, full each morning), shown as "Their HP" when hurt or where monsters live. Monsters hurt whichever of you they reach; at zero farmer two faints to the clinic with the same bill as yours (from the shared gold) while you play on. Your shoes, rings and professions protect them too (gear is shared).
- v142: farmer two is kept with the farm: the first time they join they pick a shirt and hair, and they get their own bed in the farmhouse (on free floor in the bed corner, never over furniture). When they rejoin they come back where they were, holding what they held. The day ends when both of you are in bed: whoever lies down first waits, walking gets you up, and if they leave while you wait the night comes. Passing out at 2 am still takes you both home.
- v141: a dialog belongs to whoever opened it and covers their half; the game does not pause, so the other farmer plays on. Farmer two works theirs with their controller. Only one dialog at a time; the menu still pauses both.
- v140: you go separate ways: farmer two takes map exits and doors alone and their half shows where they are. In the mine you share one floor: whoever changes floor brings the other along if they are down there. Each morning they wake beside you.
- v139: while they play the screen splits: you on the left half, them on the right, each half following its farmer (12 of the map's 24 columns at a time). Alone, the whole map shows.
- v137: not on a phone: on a touch-only screen with a short side under 600 px, the second controller's A shows a note instead of joining. Tablets, and touch screens with a mouse or trackpad, still play two.
- v136: they ride the horse too, one rider at a time (whoever gets on first), at its speed; going indoors sets them down and the horse waits outside.
- v134: they fish too, with their own A for the bite and the catch bar, one line in the water at a time (the other farmer's cast is refused while it is out). Their line holds them, not you.
- v131: their energy bar shows under yours while they play, and their own prompt (with A) floats over the tile they face. Your bed ends the day for you both: they wake beside you, rested as you are, and passing out at 2 am takes both home with 60% energy.
- v126: controllers (the browser's standard layout; fixed, not rebindable): stick or d-pad walks; A uses (held, it lifts the fishing bar); B closes a dialog or the menu or stops placing a building; X bag, Y crafting, View journal, L3 neighbours; LB and RB the previous and next hotbar slot; LT seeds, RT turn furniture, R3 whistle; Menu opens and closes the menu. In a dialog or the menu the stick or d-pad moves between buttons (left and right move a slider) and A presses. Once the pad is used, the Use prompt shows A. The menu's Controls section lists it.
- v119: every key below except Esc and ` can be changed in the menu (Controls: click a key, press the new one; Esc cancels, Backspace removes a second key, Reset keys goes back). Each action takes up to two keys. A key another action used moves to this one, and that action takes the old key; a change that would leave an action with no key is refused. Keys are kept per browser, like the sound settings, not in the save. Messages, the hotbar corners, the Use prompt and the menu buttons show the key as bound. The defaults are as listed here.
- v119: with Ctrl or Cmd held, keys stay the browser's (only Ctrl Z, undo, reaches the game), and holding a key opens a menu once rather than over and over.
- H whistles for the horse.
- R turns the furniture you face; U or Ctrl+Z (or Undo in the menu) undoes your last place, pick-up or turn today (v99). Neither touches neighbours' rooms or the world.
- Hotbar: 1 Hoe, 2 Can, 3 Rod, 4 Pick, 5 Sword, 6 Seeds, 7 Kit, 8 Axe (v19, added at the end so no key moved).
- The seed slot plants the selected seed type. Press 6 or Q again (or tap the slot again) to switch to the next type you own, in-season types first.
- The kit slot places the selected crafted object on untilled farm grass. Press 7 again to switch. Face a placed object and press Use to open it (load, collect, pick up).
- I opens the bag, C opens crafting.
- V switches the HUD between full, minimal and hidden (v112).

## Values: the Stardew scale

Numbers follow Stardew Valley's tested economy by role (v11): each item uses the scale and ratios of its closest Stardew counterpart, refitted to our growth times, with our own names. Energy is Stardew's model: 270 base, 2 per hoe, watering and pickaxe use, 8 per fishing cast. See CLAUDE.md.

### The catch bar (v41)

Stardew's bobber bar by role: after a bite, hold Use to lift your bar (it falls when you let go and bounces at the bottom). The meter starts at 30%, fills 22% a second while the fish is inside your bar and drains 30% outside; full lands the fish, empty loses it. Fish swim faster and pause less with difficulty, and from difficulty 3 they dart. A bot reacting every quarter second lands about 75 to 90% of easy fish and 40% of the hardest.

- Bar height: 28 of 120, + half the rod's and enchantment's old zone bonus, + 1.5 per fishing level, x1.3 steady hand, x1.25 cork bobber.
- Treasure (15%, doubled by the treasure hunter): keep it in the bar to fill its meter; one roll of bait, coal, ore, geodes, quartz, tidestone, the fishing book, wild bait or the tide ring.
- Trash (8% of bites, caught at once): driftwood, sodden letter, broken glass, old boot. Recycler (25 wood, 25 stone, 1 iron bar; Stardew's): driftwood to wood or coal, letter to fiber or cloth, glass to quartz, boot to stone, coal or iron ore.
- Bait (Stardew: 1 bug meat for 5; ours 1 gel for 5) halves the wait, needs the ashwood rod. Wild bait (10 fiber, 5 gel for 5): wait x0.75, 25% double catches.
- Ironwood rod (Stardew's iridium rod, 7,500g) takes tackle, 20 catches each: spinner (2 iron bars; bites 25% sooner), cork bobber (10 wood, 5 hardwood, 10 gel; bar +25%), trap bobber (1 copper bar, 10 gel; drain x0.67), lead bobber (1 iron bar, 10 stone; no bounce), treasure hunter (2 gold bars).
- Bobber styles: six colours, a new one every 25 fish.
