WASD or arrows to walk. Space uses the selected tool on the outlined tile. Keys 1 to 7 pick a tool, I opens the bag, C opens crafting, M mutes sound and music. Slot 6 holds your seeds: press 6 or Q again (or tap the slot again) to switch seed type. Slot 7 places crafted sprinklers and machines on untilled farm grass; press 7 again to switch. On a phone, use the pad or tap a tile next to you. Till, plant, water, sleep, harvest, then drop produce in the bin. Crops only grow in their season and wither when it ends. Follow the path east to the Ashgrove Wilds to forage, refill at the pond, or face the pond with the rod to fish: wait for the bite, then stop the marker in the green. The cave at the top of the Wilds leads into the Hollow: mine rocks for stone and ore, which upgrades at Bram's workbench need, and fight slimes with the sword. Face a neighbour to talk or give a gift. The path west of the farmhouse leads to the village: its quest board posts requests, and each season has a festival at the table in the square. Bram's workbench also builds a barn for cows and goats. The day ends at 2 am. Press the backquote key to show debug tools.
