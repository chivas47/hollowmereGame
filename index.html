Getting started

Till, plant, water, sleep, harvest, then drop produce in the bin by the house; it pays out overnight. Crops only grow in their season. Rain waters the farm for you, and the morning report gives the forecast. The day ends at 2 am: sleep in the farmhouse bed before then.

East of the farm are the Ashgrove Wilds: forage, refill your can at the pond, or face it with the rod to fish (stop the marker in the green). The cave there leads into the Hollow for stone, ore and slimes. West of the farmhouse is the village: neighbours, the quest board and a festival each season.
